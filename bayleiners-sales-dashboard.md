# 📊 BayLeiners Sales Dashboard (2017–2019)

## 🔎 Project Overview

The BayLeiners Sales Dashboard analyzes business performance between 2017, 2018 and 2019.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales, costs, profits, and profit margins.
- Identify top-performing products, customers, and cities.
- Examine sales channel and warehouse performance.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Customers:** 50
- **Total Orders:** 7,991
- **Total Sales:** 154.6M
- **Total Cost:** 96.8M
- **Total Profit:** 57.8M
- **Profit Margin:** 37.4%

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI:** Dashboard development and visualization.

## 💡 Key Insights

- One sales channel contributes 53.6% of total sales.
- Mobile Phones and Laptops are leading products by sales.
- The East Warehouse shows strong profitability.

## 🚀 Recommendations

- Diversify sales channels.
- Improve warehouse performance.
- Focus on products with stronger profit margins.

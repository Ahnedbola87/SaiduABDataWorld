# 📊 Stationery and Gadget Store Sales Dashboard

## 📝 Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides an overview of sales performance, profitability, customer activity, shipping costs, and employee distribution.

It is designed to help management monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify business trends, and gain insights into products, customers, suppliers, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor total sales, profit, and order volumes.
- Analyze sales distribution by category, product, supplier, and region.
- Track sales and profitability trends over time.
- Identify top-performing products, customers, and countries.
- Examine shipping modes and associated costs.
- Analyze employee distribution across regions and positions.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** Approximately $13.8M
- **Total Profit:** $3M
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 30K units
- **Average Shipping Cost:** $26
- **Total Employees:** 5,470
- **Total Customers:** 5,243

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI:** Dashboard development, data visualization, and analysis.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation. *(Include if used in this project.)*

## 📂 Dashboard Components

- **Sales Overview:** Overall sales performance and yearly trends.
- **Product Insights:** Top-performing products and suppliers.
- **Supplier & Shipping Analysis:** Supplier contributions and shipping mode analysis.
- **Customer Analysis:** Customer distribution, top customers, and country-level sales.
- **Employee Analysis:** Employee distribution by region and position.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Profitability trends and shipping cost analysis.

## 💡 Key Insights

1. Sales were distributed across different product categories, customer segments, and shipping modes.
2. Printer Paper, Pens, and Headphones were among the leading products by revenue.
3. Brazil, UK, India, USA, and China were key international markets.
4. Some customers contributed consistently to sales.
5. Average shipping cost was approximately $26.
6. Sales and profit were distributed across the regions analyzed.

## 🚀 Recommendations

- Strengthen inventory planning and marketing for high-demand products.
- Explore customer retention strategies to encourage repeat purchases.
- Maintain supplier diversity to reduce dependency on individual suppliers.
- Review shipping costs and evaluate opportunities to improve delivery efficiency.
- Use employee distribution insights to support workforce planning.
- Monitor profitability alongside shipping costs to identify opportunities for improvement.
